# Logo candidates

Six candidates in three directions, two variants each. The characters follow the `ip-as-logo` skill's rules: two character colors plus one background color, and the character rises out of a bottom corner. No image model was available (Higgsfield had 0 credits and no Gemini or OpenAI key was set), so each candidate is flat vector art drawn with Pillow, not a generated image.

| File | Idea | Corner |
|---|---|---|
| `candidate-1.png` | Ember sprite. A blunt amber flame with a cream face, for the warmth of a model running on your own GPU. Muted navy background. | lower-left |
| `candidate-2.png` | Ember sprite, darker ember flame on warm cocoa, close to the app panel. | lower-right |
| `candidate-3.png` | Quiet ghost. A cream ghost with an amber band (the overlay bar) across it, for a private presence that hovers over your chat. Deep green background. | lower-left |
| `candidate-4.png` | Quiet ghost, inverted. A charcoal ghost with an amber band on muted ember. | lower-right |
| `candidate-5.png` | Reply bubble. An amber speech bubble with a face and a dark bar on top: the bar dropping over the message box. Muted plum background. **Chosen.** | lower-left |
| `candidate-6.png` | Reply bubble, ember bubble with a dark bar on sand. | lower-right |

## Chosen: candidate 5

It is the only direction that shows the product itself: a reply (the speech bubble) with the overlay bar sitting on top of it, which is also what the name means. Amber on dark plum sits next to the app's `#1f1f21` panel and `#f2a22a` accent. At 16 px it still reads as a chat bubble with a face. The ember and the ghost also survive at 16 px, but they read as a drop and a blob with no link to chat.

The tray icon (`roverlay/assets/tray.png`) is a simplified version. It has no background, and its bar is ember with a small gap above the body, so the bar stays visible on both dark and light taskbars.
